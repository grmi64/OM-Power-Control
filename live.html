Connection & station

Tested with OM-3501A. Compatibility with other models is unverified. Other models use monitoring only; readings and the 3,500 W scale require validation. If you confirm compatibility with another model, contact the author with the model, firmware version and test results so support can be reviewed and added.

Instrument details

Power and SWR matched the amplifier in live hardware testing. SWR is calculated from forward and reflected power. The reflected-power scaling remains under validation across operating levels. Operating commands are sent once and confirmed by the amplifier. Normal cooling and warm-up cycles are preserved.
